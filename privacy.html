Foqusd Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026

Foqusd is a speed-reading app. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: Foqusd does not collect any personal data, and nothing you read is sent to us. One optional feature, which is off by default, contacts a third party; it is described under "Optional online feature" below.

What Foqusd does not do

  • It does not require an account or sign-in.
  • It does not collect your name, email, location, contacts, or device identifiers.
  • It does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking of any kind.
  • It does not send the text you read, your reading history, or your settings to us or to any third party. The only thing that can leave your device is described under "Optional online feature", and only if you turn it on.

Where your data lives

Everything you add to Foqusd (pasted text, imported .txt, .pdf, and .epub files, reading progress, statistics, and display settings) is stored only on your device, in the app's own storage and a shared container used by the Foqusd Share Extension. It is removed when you delete the app. If you use iCloud device backups, Apple may include this app data in your backup under your own Apple account; Foqusd has no access to those backups.

On-device features

  • Narration uses the speech voices built into iOS and runs entirely on your device.
  • Word definitions use the dictionary built into iOS. Lookups are handled by Apple's system and are not sent to us.
  • Importing files reads only the files you explicitly choose or share to Foqusd.
  • Sharing a web link: if you share a link to Foqusd from an app that doesn't provide the page's text, the Foqusd Share extension downloads that page directly from the website to extract its text. That request goes from your device straight to the website you chose, the same as opening it in a browser. It does not pass through any server of ours, and we never see or store it. The website's own operator may log the request, under their own policy.

Optional online feature: book cover lookup

In Reading Settings you can turn on Look up missing covers online. It is off by default. When it is on, for EPUB books that came without a cover, Foqusd sends the book's ISBN, or its title and author, directly from your device to Open Library (openlibrary.org, run by the Internet Archive) to find a cover image. Open Library can see that request and your IP address, and handles it under its own privacy policy. The request does not pass through any server of ours, and we never see or store it. Your reading text, reading history, and settings are never sent. When the setting is off, Foqusd sends nothing. You can also set a book's cover from your own photos, which stays on your device.

Purchases

The optional Foqusd Pro unlock is a one-time in-app purchase processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. Foqusd never sees your payment details. Foqusd only receives confirmation from Apple that the purchase was made, in order to unlock the feature.

Children

Because Foqusd collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.
