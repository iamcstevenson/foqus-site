Foqus Privacy Policy

Effective October 9, 2026

Foqus is a speed-reading app. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: Foqus does not collect any personal data, and nothing you read is sent to us or to any third party.

What Foqus does not do

  • It does not require an account or sign-in.
  • It does not collect your name, email, location, contacts, or device identifiers.
  • It does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking of any kind.
  • It does not send the text you read, your reading history, or your settings to us or to any third party.

Where your data lives

Everything you add to Foqus (pasted text, imported .txt, .pdf, and .epub files, reading progress, statistics, and display settings) is stored only on your device, in the app's own storage and a shared container used by the Foqus Share Extension. It is removed when you delete the app. If you use iCloud device backups, Apple may include this app data in your backup under your own Apple account; Foqus has no access to those backups.

On-device features

  • Narration uses the speech voices built into iOS and runs entirely on your device.
  • Word definitions use the dictionary built into iOS. Lookups are handled by Apple's system and are not sent to us.
  • Importing files reads only the files you explicitly choose or share to Foqus.
  • Sharing a web link: if you share a link to Foqus from an app that doesn't provide the page's text, the Foqus Share extension downloads that page directly from the website to extract its text. That request goes from your device straight to the website you chose, the same as opening it in a browser. It does not pass through any server of ours, and we never see or store it. The website's own operator may log the request, under their own policy.

Purchases

The optional Foqus Pro unlock is a one-time in-app purchase processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. Foqus never sees your payment details. Foqus only receives confirmation from Apple that the purchase was made, in order to unlock the feature.

Children

Because Foqus collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.
