Frequently asked questions

How do I add something to read?
Tap the + button in your library to paste text or import a .txt, .pdf, or .epub file. You can also use the Share button in other apps and choose Foqus.

What are Flash and Read Along?
Flash shows one word at a time with a fixed focal point. Read Along shows the full text with the current word highlighted and scrolling with you. Switch between them at the top of the reader at any time.

How do I look up a word?
In Flash, tap the word. In Read Along, press and hold a word. VoiceOver users can use the "Look up definition" action.

What does Foqus Pro unlock?
Audio narration, with a content-aware voice and a British accent where available. All reading modes and accessibility features are free. Pro is a one-time purchase, with no subscription.

I bought Pro on another device.
Open the narration paywall and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I adjust text size, themes, and spacing?
Tap the AA button in the library or reader. Options include Light, Dark, Sepia, and High Contrast themes, text size, letter and line spacing, Bionic Reading, and break reminders.
